About Berkland

Clean Spaces • Healthy Lives

Berkland Cleaning Services, a division of Babylon Cleaning Service, is a full-service residential, commercial and specialty cleaning company serving homeowners, renters, property managers and businesses across Washington, DC, Maryland and Northern Virginia.

From a weekly house cleaning to a large commercial contract, every job is handled by trained, background-checked professionals who take pride in consistent, reliable results — whether we're cleaning a family home, an office, or a specialized diplomatic facility.

We believe cleaning is personal, whether it's your home or your workplace. That's why we focus on clear communication, dependable scheduling, and consistent quality on every single visit.
