Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean homes, or only businesses?

Both — Berkland cleans houses, apartments and condos as well as offices, schools, retail and specialized facilities across the DMV.

Can I set up a recurring cleaning schedule?

Yes — choose weekly, every 2 weeks, every 4 weeks, or monthly service, and we'll keep your space consistently clean.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every home and business is different, so we provide a customized quote based on your property size, condition, frequency and the services you need.

Do you service embassies and diplomatic residences?

Yes — as a premium specialty service, with strict confidentiality procedures designed for diplomatic missions and executive residences across the DMV.

Will I get a reminder before my scheduled appointment?

Yes — an automatic reminder email is sent ahead of your appointment so you never miss a scheduled visit.
